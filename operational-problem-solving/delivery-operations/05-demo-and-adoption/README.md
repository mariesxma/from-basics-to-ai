# 05 — Help a user complete the task

**Format:** worked learning case with exercises. All organisations, data, and examples are fictional.

## Carry forward

Demonstrate the stage 4 workflow using the same six orders and the same decision rule. Do not introduce extra features just for the demo.

## Worked output: three-minute demonstration

- **First 30 seconds:** explain the dispatcher's decision, capacity of two, and the supplied snapshot.
- **Next minute:** show why A102 and A101 rank first; open their evidence rather than merely pointing at scores.
- **Next minute:** walk through assignment and explain why A104/A106 need data review.
- **Final 30 seconds:** show the action record, state the prototype limitations, and ask the user to identify one mismatch with their workflow.

This can be a paper walkthrough today. It is not evidence that real users adopted a working product.

## Quick-start card

1. Check the snapshot time and unresolved quality issues.
2. Inspect a recommendation's reasons before assigning it.
3. Select the responsible owner and follow-up time.
4. Confirm that the action was recorded once.
5. Revisit open investigations and record outcomes separately from delivery status.

## Usability task and feedback log

Ask a practice partner: “Choose and assign two investigations, then explain what you would do with the unscored orders.” Observe without coaching first.

Record: task, completion, time, errors, help needed, user quote, proposed change. Keep entries blank until observed; never invent participant feedback.

If someone reads an unscored order as safe, change its wording or placement, then repeat the task. Record the revision and why it was made.

中文：演示是解释价值，观察测试才知道用户能不能独立完成任务。不要把自己的猜测写成用户反馈。

## Your task

Give two explanations: one for a dispatcher focused on today's actions, and one for a manager focused on risk, workload, and evidence of impact.

## Gate to the next stage

Prepare a repeatable task and a feedback log. A completed observation requires an actual participant; until then its status remains planned.
