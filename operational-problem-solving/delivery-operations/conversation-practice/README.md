# Conversation practice

Practise how to move from a vague complaint to a shared decision, evidence, scope, and an achievable next commitment.

**All dialogue is fictional role-play.** It is not an interview transcript, customer evidence, or proof that any pilot or application exists. Client statements below are invented to illustrate questioning techniques. Keep them separate from actual findings in your own notes.

所有对话都是模拟练习，不是真实客户记录。英文台词可以直接练习；中文注释解释为什么这样问。

## Start here

1. Read the [annotated conversations](TRANSCRIPT.md) aloud with a practice partner.
2. Use the [talking-point playbook](TALKING-POINTS.md) to practise alternative responses.
3. Close the script and summarise the problem, evidence gaps, agreed scope, and next deliverable in your own words.

A real engagement takes several conversations. Do not compress discovery, validation, adoption, and evaluation into one sales pitch.

## Roles

- **Facilitator:** investigates the problem and helps shape a useful next step.
- **Maya, operations manager:** accountable for operational priorities and staffing decisions.
- **Leo, dispatcher:** knows the day-to-day workflow and can explain concrete examples.
- **Sam, data owner:** knows source definitions, availability, and limitations.

All names are fictional. One person can play multiple roles during practice, but do not assume a manager knows every detail of the operator's workflow.
